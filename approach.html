Our Approach

At M.R. Morarka GDC Rural Research Foundation, we are committed to the responsible and efficient use of resources, measurable outcomes, and accountability to our supporters and partners, and above all to women and underprivileged sections of society. We value innovation and new ideas, recognizing that field-based innovations provide hope and tangible pathways toward sustainable development for our stakeholders and beneficiaries. We therefore strive continuously to develop creative and practical solutions that improve the lives of the most vulnerable communities.

We believe that the quality of life in remote and disadvantaged areas is largely shaped by livelihood opportunities, the literacy levels of women and children, the effective and optimal use of locally available natural resources, and the capacity building of human resources and institutions. Progress in any one of these areas reinforces growth in the others, creating a virtuous cycle of sustainability and holistic community development. Accordingly, M.R. Morarka GDC Rural Research Foundation ensures that all its interventions contribute to the goal of Sustainable Integrated Development.
