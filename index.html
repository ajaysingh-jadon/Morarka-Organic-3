Genesis of Morarka Organic

Organic Taste You’ll Love.

Established in 2005 under the umbrella of the M.R. Morarka-GDC Rural Research Foundation, Morarka Organic Food Limited (MOFL) was founded with a clear commitment to promote sustainable agriculture and strengthen farmer livelihoods across India.

More than just food, we deliver trust. By working directly with farming communities, we ensure fair practices, authentic sourcing, and consistent quality. The result is clean, nutritious, and naturally flavorful food you can enjoy every day with confidence.

Down To Earth

Down To Earth, from the house of Morarka Organic, represents a refined approach to organic living. Crafted through more than 12 years of research and adherence to the highest organic certification standards, the brand delivers food that is pure, authentic, and thoughtfully sourced from nature.
Every product reflects a commitment to quality, sustainability, and mindful nourishment—bringing the finest organic ingredients from farm to table.
