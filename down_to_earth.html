DOWN TO EARTH

NURTURING FARMS & NOURISHING PEOPLE

Down to Earth label is a vision of the single largest Organic farmer support programme in the world by Morarka Organic. Spanning 20 years, we've helped farmers across India embrace a sustainable future by nurturing 2,50,000 acres of land under organic cultivation. Our initiatives have ensured higher yields, healthier farms & sustainable life for the farmer.

Down to Earth label has over 300 plus varieties of wholesome, better tasting food. Nourished by nature, our food has natural nutrients that can keep you energized so that you can have a better day, every day.

From Farm to Retail - Down to Earth

From being in the back end of the value chain for over 10 years, Morarka Organic Foods Limited has now got into the front end, i.e., retailing of certified organic products under its own brand 'Down to Earth'.

While this initiative is expected to provide direct linkages to the thousands of certified organic producers, it will also enable quality conscious consumers to access the very best quality of food deserved by them at reasonable prices.

India's Answer to the Global Organic Revolution

Down to Earth is expected to be India's answer to the global organic revolution. It is dedicated to bringing people back to the organic way of life.

Down to Earth provides large range of food products- cereals, pulses, spices, condiments, fruits, vegetables, oils, dairy products, Ready-To-Eat snacks and much more-amounting to almost 90 percent of the Indian kitchen grocery.
