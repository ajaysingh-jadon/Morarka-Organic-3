Our Roots

A three-decade legacy of sustainable development, agricultural innovation, and social responsibility

Morarka Organic Food Limited is deeply rooted in a legacy of sustainable development, social responsibility, and agricultural innovation that began over three decades ago with the establishment of The Morarka Foundation.

Founded in 1993 by Mr. Kamal Morarka in memory of his late father Mr. M.R. Morarka, The Morarka Foundation was conceived as a non-profit, voluntary organization and a corporate social responsibility initiative of Gannon Dunkerley & Company Ltd.

Since its inception, the Foundation has been committed to creating lasting value for the nation through innovations in natural resource management, sustainable agriculture, and the capacity building of farming communities.

Innovation in Organic Agriculture

To address this challenge, the Foundation pioneered on-farm fertility management practices based on vermiculture technology. The large-scale adoption of this innovation by more than 100,000 farmers within three years positioned the Foundation as the world's largest producer of vermi-cast at the time.

This breakthrough received national recognition, earning awards for excellence in technology innovation from the Ministry of Science and Technology, Government of India, in 2001 and 2002.

The success of these initiatives naturally led to compliance with organic standards and laid the foundation for one of India's largest organic agriculture development programmes. Over the years, the Foundation also evolved pro-biotic and biological approaches to pest and fertility management, strengthening the sustainability of organic cultivation.

Certification & Traceability

Recognizing the importance of certification and traceability, the Foundation introduced IT-enabled group certification systems, aligned with international organic standards. Parallelly, it promoted rural entrepreneurship in organic agri-business and facilitated direct market linkages between producers and traders.

Genesis of Morarka Organic

To further strengthen the connection between farmers and consumers, Morarka Organic Food Limited was established in 2006–07 as the marketing arm of The Morarka Foundation. The company was envisioned to create transparent, ethical, and efficient value chains—ensuring that farmers receive a greater share of consumer spending while delivering authentic organic products.

Expanding Impact Across India

Between 1995 and 2004, Morarka initiatives reached tens of thousands of farm families across Rajasthan, Gujarat, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Haryana, West Bengal, Kerala, the North-East, and other regions. Programmes ranged from agricultural extension, integrated watershed development, drought-proofing, and soil conservation to post-disaster livelihood restoration in earthquake-affected regions such as Kutch, Gujarat.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Corporate Philosophy

At Morarka Organic, our philosophy is rooted in fairness, innovation, and responsibility.

Fairness

We strive to increase the producer's share in the consumer value chain through ethical business practices, technology-led solutions, and transparent market linkages.

Innovation

By working at the grassroots, we reach and empower lakhs of small and marginal farmers, encourage innovation, and inspire professionals to contribute meaningfully to sustainable development.

Responsibility

In an era where food systems have become complex and often disconnected from nature, Morarka Organic seeks to restore balance—bringing people closer to the earth, reviving the principles of clean and healthy living.
