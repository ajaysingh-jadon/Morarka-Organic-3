Our Roots

EST. 1993

A three-decade legacy of sustainable development, agricultural innovation, and social responsibility

Morarka Organic Foods Limited is deeply rooted in a legacy of sustainable development, social responsibility, and agricultural innovation that began over three decades ago with the establishment of The M.R. Morarka-GDC Rural Research Foundation.

In the memory of his father, Late Mr. M. R. Morarka, the M.R. Morarka-GDC Rural Research Foundation was founded in 1993 by Shri Kamal Morarka. Conceived as a non-profit, voluntary organization, the Foundation was established as part of the corporate social responsibility initiative of Gannon Dunkerley & Company Ltd.

Since its inception, the Foundation remained steadfast in its commitment to creating enduring value for the nation through innovation in natural resource management, the promotion of sustainable agriculture, and the strengthening of capacities within farming communities. Its work reflected a long-term vision of inclusive growth and rural resilience.

1993

Foundation Incepted

The M.R. Morarka-GDC Rural Research Foundation established as a non-profit, voluntary organization, committed to creating lasting value through innovations in natural resource management and sustainable agriculture.

1995-2004

Nationwide Expansion

Reached tens of thousands of farm families across Rajasthan, Gujarat, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Haryana, West Bengal, Kerala, and the North-East. Introduced organic inputs and reduced dependency on chemical fertilizers.

2001-2002

National Recognition

Received prestigious awards for excellence in technology innovation from the Ministry of Science and Technology, Government of India, for pioneering vermiculture technology adoption.

2006-07

Morarka Organic Established

Morarka Organic Foods Limited established as the marketing arm of The M.R. Morarka-GDC Rural Research Foundation, creating transparent, ethical, and efficient value chains connecting farmers to consumers.

Present

Farm-to-Table Organic Ecosystem

Empowering 100,000+ farmers, once recognized as the world’s largest producer of vermi-compost, the initiative now supports lakhs of small and marginal farmers through organic agriculture programmes. It focuses on capacity building, monitoring, and technical support while linking farmers’ produce to bulk, B2B, and B2C markets. A strong network of professionals in quality control, cleaning, procurement, certifications, and R&D ensures proper processing. This integrated effort delivers pure, trustworthy, ready-to-cook organic products from farm to consumer.
