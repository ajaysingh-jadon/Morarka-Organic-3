Strategic Evolution

Activities & Operations

Morarka Organic Foods Limited began with direct procurement of organic produce from farms and its supply to organic trade partners. Over time, operations expanded to include year-round storage, enabling greater aggregation of farm-level produce to meet market demand. Responding to the growing need for processed organic foods, MOFL established in-house and partner processing facilities and subsequently developed its own distribution and retail network across India.

From an initial range of select cereals, pulses, oilseeds, and spices, MOFL today offers a diversified portfolio of over 170 certified organic products.

Integrated Value Chain

Cultivation, Storage, Processing & Value Addition

Morarka Organic Foods Limited (MOFL) has expanded organic cultivation across multiple agro-climatic zones of India through strong technical expertise and farmer engagement.

  • Built an integrated, end-to-end organic value chain covering cultivation, storage, processing, and value addition.
  • Strengthened value chain capabilities with farmer-friendly tools, small-scale processing solutions, and robust organic storage systems to meet rising demand for value-added products.
  • Addressed the lack of organic-compliant technologies through intensive in-house R&D.
  • Introduced chemical-free farm-level storage using airtight metal bins and masonry units, adopted by 10,000+ farmers.
  • Developed advanced CO₂ fumigation technology for large-scale organic storage, positioning MOFL among the first three organizations globally to master this method.
  • Innovated solar-based dehydration and other crop-specific processing technologies & machineries.
  • Developed 30+ organic processing technologies in the last three years.
  • Continuously enhancing packaging, cold warehouse storage, and logistics to ensure certified organic produce with consistent quality, safety, and shelf stability from farm to market—improving overall product shelf life.
Strategic Alliances

Collaborations & Entrepreneurial Partnerships

Anchored in a public-private partnership approach, MOFL has built strategic collaborations with individuals and organizations at both national and international levels. A unique model was developed to engage educated, unemployed youth from producer communities to establish and manage key value-chain functions including cultivation, certification, procurement, processing, storage, distribution, and retail.

Additionally, partnerships with high-capacity and high-quality processing units have strengthened operational efficiency. Within the first five years, MOFL successfully collaborated with over 300 entrepreneurs across India, significantly expanding its organic footprint.
